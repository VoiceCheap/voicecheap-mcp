# VoiceCheap

Use the VoiceCheap skill in `skills/voicecheap/SKILL.md` for audio/video and
YouTube transcription, subtitles and chapters, speech and permitted voice
creation, images and thumbnails, dubbing and lip sync, or authorized publishing
and workspace work. Discover the current tools and their schemas; never infer
permission from the presence of this extension.

Authenticate with `/mcp auth voicecheap` when the server requests browser OAuth.
The user completes sign-in and chooses the permissions. Do not ask for
credentials in conversation. Start with an authorized account read and only the
additional permissions needed for the requested workflow.

Generation uses the user's applicable existing credits or allowances and requires
their request.
Publishing needs the user's destination and privacy choice. Voice cloning needs
explicit speaker permission. Keep account identifiers, private media and
credentials out of public listings and demos; use synthetic review content.
The extension has no trusted tool bypass and does not automatically approve actions.
